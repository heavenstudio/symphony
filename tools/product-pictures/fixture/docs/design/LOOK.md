# The look

How Orchard looks: its type, its colour and the recipe card.

## Type

Headings are set in a serif and the steps of a recipe in a sans at 18 px, read at arm's length
from a kitchen counter. Quantities show in grams and millilitres first, with cups beside them.

## The recipe card

A recipe's card shows its picture, its name, its cooking time and how many readers cooked it.
